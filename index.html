The Origins of a Builder

Long before university, I learned business not from textbooks, but through direct experience. I didn't know the terminology, but I was learning the concepts.

Age 9

Selling Comic Books at School

I obtained free comic books and sold them to students. I quickly noticed that scarce volumes commanded higher prices (₦30 to ₦100).

"I could take an opportunity that already existed, add distribution, and create value from it."

Early Teens

Building a Distribution System

I started selling a cold drink at school. As demand grew, I recruited other students, offering them one free drink for every four sold.

"I was moving from being a salesperson to managing a small distribution system: source → prepare → recruit → distribute → sell → reinvest."

What I Have Built

From fintech infrastructure to AI search optimization, my projects are different, but the underlying pattern remains consistent.

Current

Dala Forge

AI / Search / Business Intelligence

Dala Forge grew out of a problem I encountered repeatedly: AI systems often do not understand businesses and their products deeply enough to guide customers correctly.

I build solutions across two layers: AI Discovery (ensuring AI systems can find and recommend a company) and AI Product Understanding (ensuring the AI can accurately explain the product and its local constraints).

  • Built my first real website from scratch using AI as a development tool.
  • Developed the Abuja AI Visibility Index, a research project systematically testing why AI mentions certain real estate companies over others.
In Development

CodeLens

AI / Software Development Environment

Current AI coding tools assume users understand code. CodeLens explores a different possibility: What if people could interact with software in Plain English while maintaining precise control over the underlying source code?

Built with Python and a web interface, it translates natural-language instructions into explicit source-code changes.

2021 – 2025

Naira Bridge

Blockchain / Fintech Infrastructure

My first serious attempt at building a technology company. The goal was to create a regulated infrastructure layer between cryptocurrency platforms and Nigeria's financial system, potentially utilizing the eNaira ecosystem.

Without funding, a technical co-founder, or coding skills, I recruited a small remote team (frontend, backend, blockchain) on an equity basis and navigated complex regulatory conversations with individuals familiar with the CBN.

"The project taught me that an idea can be technically interesting and address a real problem, while the surrounding regulatory and infrastructure environment still makes it impractical."

The Engineering Mindset

My formal education is in Civil Engineering at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto, including industrial training with the Federal Capital Development Authority.

Engineering teaches me to think in terms of systems, constraints, dependencies, failure modes, and practical implementation.

I do not see engineering, software, and entrepreneurship as separate paths. They all require understanding how systems work and what happens when those systems meet real-world constraints.

The Pattern

Across all my projects, the thing I value most is not that every idea succeeded. It is that I keep moving through a deliberate loop:

1 Idea & Investigation
2 Gathering People & Resources
3 Prototype & Evidence
4 Iteration

"I have spent years developing the willingness to pursue an idea far enough to discover what is actually standing in its way."
